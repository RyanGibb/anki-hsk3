# Pinyin convention for the HSK 3.0 grammar example sentences

Base standard: 汉语拼音正词法基本规则 (GB/T 16159), with the departures for 一/不 noted
below, which follow HSK textbook practice.

## 1. Word grouping (where the spaces go)

Spaces separate **words**, not syllables. A word is written solid.

- Compounds are one word: `túshūguǎn`, `zìxíngchē`, `niánqīngrén`, `yuèláiyuè`.
- Numeral + measure word are separate: `yí ge`, `zhè jiàn`, `liǎng běn shū`, `sān cì`.
- Verb + object are separate: `chī fàn`, `kāi chē`, `shàng kè`, `huí jiā`.
  BUT where the pair has become a single lexical item, keep it solid: `chànggē`,
  `shuìjiào`, `bāngmáng`.
- Verb + aspect particle is solid: `qùguo`, `chīle` — EXCEPT where 了 ends a clause,
  where it stands alone: `tā lái le.`
- Verb + resultative complement is solid: `tīngdào`, `xuéhǎo`.
- Verb + directional complement is solid, and the complement goes neutral: `zǒuchūqu`,
  `kūqǐlai`, `jiānchíxiàqu`, `huànhuíqu`. Standing alone as the main verb it keeps its
  tone: `tā chūqù le`, `huílái ba`.
- Reduplication is solid: `kànkan`, `shuōshuo`, `mànmānr`.
- Suffixes attach: `rénmen`, `zhuōzi`, `yìdiǎnr`, `wǒmen`.
- 的/地/得 stand alone: `wǒ de shū`, `mànmàn de zǒu`, `pǎo de kuài`.
- Adverb + adjective are separate: `hěn dà`, `fēicháng hǎo`, `bù duì`.
- Set phrases and chengyu are solid: `qiānchángguàdù`, `zìxiāngmáodùn`.
- Longer than four syllables, split at the natural boundary: `Hànyǔ Shuǐpíng Kǎoshì`.

## 2. Tones

Write tone marks on the vowel, not tone numbers.

- **Third-tone sandhi is NOT written.** `nǐ hǎo`, `wǒ hěn hǎo`, `shuǐguǒ` — even though
  the first syllable is said as second tone.
- **一 and 不 sandhi IS written**, as HSK textbooks print it:
  - 一 before 4th tone → `yí`: `yí ge`, `yí xià`, `yíyàng`
  - 一 before 1st/2nd/3rd → `yì`: `yì tiān`, `yì nián`, `yìqǐ`, `yìdiǎnr`
  - 一 alone, counting, or final → `yī`: `yī`, `dì yī`, `yī yuè`, `xīngqīyī`
  - 不 before 4th tone → `bú`: `bú shì`, `bú duì`, `búyòng`
  - 不 elsewhere → `bù`: `bù hǎo`, `bù néng`, `bù chī`
- **Neutral tone** carries no mark: `le`, `de`, `ma`, `ne`, `ba`, `men`, `zi`, `tou`.
  Lexical neutrals too: `xiānsheng`, `péngyou`, `xǐhuan`, `yīfu`, `dìdi`, `māma`,
  `zhīdao`, `piàoliang`, `xuésheng`, `shénme`, `zěnme`.
  `个` as a measure word is neutral: `yí ge rén`.

## 3. Capitals

- First letter of the sentence.
- Proper nouns — people, places, organisations, languages, nationalities, festivals:
  `Xiǎo Gāo`, `Běijīng`, `Shànghǎi`, `Zhōngguó`, `Zhōngwén`, `Hànyǔ`, `Chūn Jié`.
  A surname and given name are separate words, each capitalised: `Wáng Xiǎomíng`.
  A title attached to a surname is lower case and separate: `Wáng lǎoshī`, `Lǐ xiānsheng`.
- Nothing else. Not 我, not the start of a clause after a comma.

## 4. Apostrophes

Insert `'` before a syllable starting with a, o or e where the break would otherwise be
ambiguous: `Xī'ān`, `nǚ'ér`, `píng'ān`, `Tiān'ānmén`.

## 5. Erhua

Write as a bare `r` on the preceding syllable: `nǎr`, `yìdiǎnr`, `wánr`, `xiǎoháir`.
Never `ér` as a separate syllable.

## 6. Punctuation

Convert Chinese punctuation to ASCII, spaced as in English:

| Chinese | pinyin |
|---|---|
| 。 | `.` |
| ， | `,` |
| 、 | `,` |
| ？ | `?` |
| ！ | `!` |
| ：| `:` |
| ；| `;` |
| 、| `,` |
| （）| `()` |
| 《》| omit, or use italics if the title is a word |
| ……  | `...` |

No space before punctuation, one space after.

## 7. What to do when the sentence is ambiguous

Some sentences are genuinely ambiguous out of context — 得 as `de`/`dé`/`děi`,
了 as `le`/`liǎo`, 还 as `hái`/`huán`, 长 as `cháng`/`zhǎng`, 教 as `jiāo`/`jiào`.
The grammar point the sentence illustrates is given alongside it: use it. If it is
still ambiguous after that, pick the reading a beginner would most likely intend and
flag the sentence.

## 8. Cases the first pass raised

- Noun + localiser stays separate and the localiser keeps its tone: `zhuōzi shàng`,
  `shūdiàn lǐ`, `fángjiān lǐ`, `dì shàng`, `shìjiè shàng`. It is a word in its own
  right, not a suffix.
  But the two-syllable localisers are single words with a neutral second syllable:
  `lǐbian`, `wàibian`, `zuǒbian`, `yòubian`, `qiánmian`, `hòumian`.
  Demonstrative + 里 fuses and keeps the tone: `zhèlǐ`, `nàlǐ`, `nǎlǐ`.
  国外 is a word: `guówài`.
- Verb-object: 看病 `kànbìng`, 说话 `shuōhuà`, 生病 `shēngbìng` are solid;
  上课/下课/上班/下班 stay separate: `shàng kè`, `xià bān`.
- 大学生 is `dàxuéshēng` — full tone, unlike standalone `xuésheng`.
- 汉字 is a common noun, lower case: `hànzì`. 汉语/中文 are capitalised.
- Numbers group at thousand/hundred/ten boundaries: `liǎngqiān sānbǎi wǔshí`.
